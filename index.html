GOOD TO KNOW

THE DETAILS.

How much does a tattoo cost?

Starting prices are $250 for small, $450 for medium, $700 for large tattoos and $1,000 for a full-day session. Final pricing depends on design, detail, size and placement.

How do I book?

Submit the booking form with your idea, placement, approximate size and preferred date. We'll contact you with a quote and availability.

Do you create custom designs?

Yes. Send your idea, preferred style, placement and size. We'll discuss the design with you.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A 50% deposit is required to secure an appointment once the design and quote have been agreed.

Is my appointment confirmed when I submit the form?

No. The form sends a request. Your appointment is confirmed only after the studio contacts you, agrees the details and receives the required deposit.
